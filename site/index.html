Capabilities

Everything You Need to Go Hands-Free

SmartGestureOS integrates deeply with Windows without requiring any special hardware or drivers.

🖱️

Precision Mouse Control

Index finger controls the cursor with 1€ filter smoothing. Pinch to click, hold to drag, two-finger scroll.

📻

Media Mode

Play/pause, skip tracks, and mute — all without touching your keyboard. Snap into Media mode instantly.

🎨

Air Drawing

Switch to Draw mode and paint in the air. Undo, redo, cycle colors, save to PNG — with hand gestures only.

🚀

App Launcher

Open Chrome, VS Code, Calculator, and more. Volume up/down, screenshot, lock PC — all mapped to gestures.

🧠

Custom Gesture Trainer

Train your own gestures with a few samples. Translation-, rotation-, and scale-invariant normalization ensures robustness.

🔒

Private by Design

Zero network access. Camera frames never touch disk. No account. No telemetry. Runs on your CPU/GPU only.

100% Local. Zero Data Collected.

SmartGestureOS processes everything on your device. Your camera frames are never saved or transmitted. There is no internet connection, no account, no analytics, and no telemetry of any kind. Read the full Privacy Policy →
