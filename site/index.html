Control Windows
With Your Hands

SmartGestureOS turns your webcam into a gesture controller. Move the mouse, click, scroll, open apps — no touching required. A desktop utility for Windows, with hand tracking on your device and no account required.

Capabilities

A New Way to Interact With Windows

Explore touchless interaction using a normal webcam. SmartGestureOS adds another way to control your PC alongside your mouse and keyboard.

🖱️

Mouse Control

Point to move a smoothed cursor. Pinch to click, pinch twice to double-click, hold to drag, and use two fingers to scroll.

📻

Media Mode

Play/pause, skip tracks, and mute. Switch to MEDIA using the Call Me gesture.

🎨

Air Drawing

Switch to Draw mode and paint in the air. Undo, redo, cycle colors, save to PNG — with hand gestures only.

🚀

App Launcher

Open Chrome or VS Code. Adjust volume, take a screenshot, or lock the PC with mapped gestures.

🧠

Learn and Personalize

Use Gesture Coach to learn the built-in poses. The experimental Trainer lets you save your own gesture samples.

🔒

Private by Design

Hand tracking runs locally. Camera frames are processed in memory; the app has no account or analytics feature.

How It Works

From Your Hand to a Windows Action

A computer-vision and human-computer interaction project. SmartGestureOS runs on Windows; it is not a separate operating system or a certified accessibility or medical solution.

  1. Webcam frame
  2. MediaPipe · 21 landmarks
  3. Raw and stable gesture
  4. Mode mapping
  5. Timed interaction
  6. Windows action

The camera, detector, classifier, mapper, event engine, controllers, and interface each have a separate role. The dashboard shows the preview, landmarks, gestures, confidence, mode, automation state, recent actions, and performance.

Getting Started

A Windows PC and a Webcam

System requirements

Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit, with a working webcam and camera access enabled for desktop apps. Keep one hand clearly visible with even lighting.

Chrome and VS Code must already be installed for their launch gestures. Brightness support depends on your display. Performance varies by camera and PC.

Your first session

  1. Launch SmartGestureOS and select your camera in Settings if needed.
  2. Place one hand in view and check that the 21 landmarks follow it.
  3. Open Gesture Coach, then try Pointing and a short Pinch.
  4. Use Call Me to explore MEDIA and DRAW.

Pause whenever you need

Press Ctrl + Alt + G or select Pause in the dashboard. Desktop actions stop while the camera preview can continue.

After resuming, lower your hand briefly to re-arm control. Tracking loss and pause release held mouse buttons.

🛡️

Local Hand Processing

SmartGestureOS processes camera frames in memory on your device. The app has no account or analytics service. MediaPipe says its Tasks APIs send performance and usage metrics to Google. Install on Windows

  1. Open the latest release and download its Windows installer and SHA256SUMS.txt.
  2. Check the release notes and checksum, run the installer, and choose your shortcuts.
  3. Launch SmartGestureOS from the Start menu and allow camera access. Python is not required.

Windows may show SmartScreen for an unsigned installer. Check the publisher, source, and checksum before deciding whether to continue.
